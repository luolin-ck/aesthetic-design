## Earlier source-and-criteria review — 2026-11-05

At that earlier source-and-criteria stage, no additional whole-style mismatch was recorded. The later selection review above found that several correctly identified images were still poor comparators. The following narrower defects were corrected:

- **Source claims were conflated.** Every asset’s association_evidence was rewritten by source. The Musica Viva record now cites its own MoMA 7233 entry rather than Hofmann’s MoMA 7726 poster. Design/model history, another museum’s physical example, general movement context and the inspected image are explicitly separated.
- **Swiss Style had an overbroad exclusion.** Expressive title lettering can itself organize the main message and image. The guide now limits “local borrowing” to cases where Swiss-like information rules actually remain confined to a footer or other small area.
- **Memphis had inconsistent requirements across guides.** The Memphis criteria and Maximalism comparison no longer require overall asymmetry, eccentric support, actual laminate or historical group membership for contemporary formal attribution. The corresponding Chinese guides and skill index were updated.
- **Three image descriptions went beyond the view.** LCM supports are described as slender round metal, without inferring a hollow section; the monochrome imperial robe description no longer invents a clear collar band; the Blakeney room describes potted plants rather than hanging planters. Oia’s image description uses stepped settlement rather than treating an unseen coastline as directly observed.
- **The Graphite date lacked the cited support.** The existing Apple source establishes availability in July 2000, not a first-introduction date in 1999. The record and style note now state only the supported period fact and leave the photographed unit’s manufacturing date unknown.
- **A scholarly figure was overinterpreted.** Indexed book text supports discussion of Floral Shoppe in a vaporwave context, but the PDF figure image was not inspected. It is not used to confirm the exact faceless-cover version or its first-use date. The artist’s downloaded cover remains the source for the selected image.

Source facts, visible observations and editorial comparisons remain separate. Physical authentication, hidden construction, unseen surfaces, live-site DOM/interactions and a blind model-recognition benchmark were not performed.

## Easily confused groups

| Group | What distinguishes the actual examples | Limit to preserve |
|---|---|---|
| Bauhaus / Modernism / De Stijl / Swiss | Documented school/work lineage; architectural volume/component relations; interdependent planar fields; information and typographic hierarchy. | These are not four exclusive color/geometry boxes. Fagus precedes and anticipates Bauhaus; Swiss posters may use diagonal structures and serif details. |
| Minimalism / Wabi-sabi / Japandi | Serial units/voids or broader reduction; restrained individual surface/form plus separate time/practice evidence; a named Japanese–Danish project with coordinated furniture and thresholds. | Beige, wood, Japanese authorship, age or a crack cannot select among them. A symmetrical, partly glossy Raku bowl need not be excluded. |
| Song objects / Literati ink / Ming furniture / Neo-Chinese | Distinct historical and medium-specific traditions; later translation needs an identifiable retained relationship and a contemporary reorganization. | The Wen album is Ming; Ming-style is not limited to pre-1644 manufacture. A contemporary Chinese facade or ancient object photo alone does not establish Neo-Chinese design. |
| Classical / Renaissance / Neoclassical | Shared classical vocabulary plus separately documented original/revival dates and branch-specific spatial/component relations. | A round arch, column, white surface or carving does not settle a period. |
| Gothic / Baroque / Rococo / Neoclassical | Stone/glass/vault relations; coordinated theatrical figure/architecture/light; curling/spreading relief; classical motifs held within a slender component outline. | Gold, ornament quantity, symmetry or absence of carving are not exclusive membership tests. |
| Bohemian / Maximalism / Memphis | Collected heterogeneous furnishing relationships; deliberately expansive texture/scale layering; documented geometric component and surface experiments. | Read the actual room/object. Carlton is broadly bilateral; sparse wall areas do not disqualify a maximalist installation. These directions may overlap. |
| Cyberpunk / Retrofuturism / Y2K / Vaporwave / Steampunk | Cinema-world context and technology/social scale; later reconstruction of an earlier imagined future; a period hardware strand; a named current album-cover collage; contemporary retro-technical modding. | Neon, chrome, age, pink or brass are search leads. A historical future proposal is not automatically a later retrofuturist reconstruction. |
| Direct Web Brutalism / Neobrutalism / Antidesign | Copeland’s authored direct-document route differs from Gumroad’s hard functional field boundaries. | The two screenshots establish different branches, not one combined recipe. Neither establishes intentional Antidesign, DOM implementation or live interactions; no fixed shadow angle is required. |

## Remaining limitations

A single view cannot cover every branch or medium. Historic identity, visible language, actual influence and quality remain separate questions. Sparse/generic neighboring explanations remain legitimate where evidence cannot distinguish them. The review does not establish dates or construction of newly supplied objects, certify religious/philosophical commitments, grant image rights or claim a blind classification score. The source pages are retained as provenance links and concise factual context, not full article caches.
