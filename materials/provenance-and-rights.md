# Provenance and Image Rights

> **Public distribution availability:** This is the conservative public edition: 9 bundled images, 34 withheld, with 28 of 36 directions lacking local visual comparators. Historical visual-review notes below describe earlier inspected source images, not a claim that withheld images are present or newly inspected. Check `availability` in catalog/records and the [publication policy](publication-policy.json). Do not download missing files automatically or infer visual confirmation from text.

## What is stored

The images are externally published photographs, reproductions or recorded screenshots of named actual works. The package’s English observations are editorial analysis of those exact images, not primary-source quotations, full cached articles, or independent authentication. The former authored SVG/PNG boards, fictional positive/negative examples, duplicate research-note cards and translated material copies have been removed.

Each entry in [catalog.json](catalog.json) points to the actual local file and a JSON record containing its work credit and dates, image credit, public source page and image URL, retrieval method, source verification scope, stated rights and attribution, visible features and limitations, file dimensions, byte count and SHA-256. No login, paywall, disabled download facility or access control was bypassed. The manifest covers package files except itself.

## Three distinct rights categories

- `public_domain`: the provider identifies this image as public domain. Met records also rely on its [Open Access policy](https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access); that policy does not cover every image on the museum’s site.
- `open_license`: the provider identifies a particular photo license such as CC BY or CC BY-SA. Preserve creator credit, source, license version and applicable conditions. The photographed design or artwork can have separate rights; do not silently extend a photo license to them.
- `restricted_public_preview`: the provider publishes a visible image but no open reuse license was established, or it states retained rights. The local preview is a comparative reference with its existing credit. This package grants no general redistribution, client-deliverable, publication or commercial-use permission.

Original provider statements and context are retained per record. A public URL, local possession, museum collection ownership, ancient depicted object, or classification result is not itself a reuse license. Do not infer that a depicted modern design is unprotected because a photographer or repository licensed the photograph.

## Evidence boundaries

The records distinguish original design/work dates, photographed-object manufacture dates, photo dates, screenshot/version dates and retrieval dates where known. Unknown values stay unknown. The current official Floral Shoppe cover’s exact design date is not established by the album’s release date. The Tsingpu project documents Chinese courtyard relationships reorganized for contemporary use without claiming its architects adopted the commercial Neo-Chinese label. Ningbo Museum and Ginkaku-ji were removed after the full per-image selection review; Tsingpu and the Raku bowl remain stronger references for those directions. The distant palace and Hollein filming-scene views were removed earlier. Retrofuturism now uses the officially licensed Pip-Boy replica image, with its later-reconstruction context documented separately from its visible controls and housing.

Read `reference_role`, `claim_scope`, `association_evidence` and `confusion_check` before applying an image. Each association_evidence entry states only the fact supported by that URL; observations and formal comparisons are editorial analysis in separate fields. A source for another example of a model does not establish the photographed object’s maker or construction. Indexed scholarly text or a figure caption does not establish an unseen figure’s exact image version. The [correspondence review](correspondence-review.md) records nearby explanations; the [full selection review](selection-review.md) records a visual decision for every image. Role is independent of image rights: neither a public-domain image nor a famous work automatically becomes a discriminating style reference.

Do not treat an author’s page, museum caption or classification in this library as evidence of a newly supplied object’s maker, age, authorization, hidden construction, fabrication process, cultural identity, psychological state or actual influence. Real examples still need scoped comparison against neighboring explanations. One view does not represent every medium or branch.

## Add or replace material

Use a named actual work with an accessible source page and a clearly identified image. Inspect the downloaded bytes before writing observations. A documented work is not automatically a useful comparator. Choose a view where the branch-defining relationships are visible at a readable size, with adequate exposure and minimal obstruction or unrelated salient content. Tourists, vehicles, display stacks, filming equipment, portraits and sensational headlines can dominate perception. Replace or exclude a weak view rather than using attribution or a long caveat to compensate; preserve genuine published images and disclose any remaining limits. Preserve the provider’s public thumbnail or image unchanged and record transformations already made by that provider. Do not substitute generated teaching images, generic decorative stock or an anonymous visual selected only for its palette. If identity, branch association or rights remain uncertain, state the uncertainty instead of manufacturing a positive label.

Update the style mapping, asset record, local note and integrity manifest together. The [offline validator](../scripts/validate_offline.py) checks package consistency with network calls disabled. Passing it is not an accuracy benchmark or legal clearance.
