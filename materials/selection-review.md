## Standard applied

Judge the actual image, separately from its attribution: defining relationships must be visible together, at readable scale and exposure, without obstruction or unrelated content dominating the view. Compare the nearest plausible explanation. A famous work or reliable source does not compensate for a weak view.

Designed complexity remains evidence: maximalist layers, Art Nouveau curves, a cyberpunk sign/infrastructure network and consumer imagery within Pop Art must not be stripped away as noise. Minor ordinary context can remain when it neither covers nor competes with the defining relationships. This does not require every real building to be photographed on an empty white field. One inspected branch remains one branch, not all media of a style.

Every replacement preserves a real-work provider image unchanged, except that the Dawnridge photograph was extracted as its native unchanged RGB JPEG from a public publisher PDF and matched to its rendered page. No package-generated replacements, local retouching, distraction removal or synthetic positive/negative boards were used. A published product visual’s camera/render method remains unknown unless separately documented. Exact credits, dates, rights and remaining limitations live in each current record. Rights from an old image never transfer to a different one.

## Coverage and limitations

The two removed context views were Ningbo Museum and Ginkaku-ji; Tsingpu courtyard views and the Raku bowl keep their respective directions covered. Replacement images receive new asset IDs, linked to the old IDs and hashes in the ledger; style/guide mappings remain stable. Object identity, dates, sources, licensing and image hashes were rebuilt for the actual new work/version. The robe is now Cleveland 1983.32, not the previous Met twelve-symbol robe; B3 is MoMA 229.1934, with its own date and attributed manufacturer. Industrial space is now LocHal, and Retrofuturism uses a licensed Pip-Boy replica rather than an actor-centered production still.

This is a qualitative image-selection audit. It does not authenticate physical objects, test functions or hidden materials, establish copyright clearance, test live interfaces, measure recognition accuracy, or promise coverage of every regional/medium branch. The offline validators check integrity and consistency; their passing result is a different kind of evidence.
