This is the single MoMA accession 229.1934, catalogued 1927–1928, not the three later display chairs in the replaced photo. MoMA attributes the manufacturer to Standard Möbel rather than establishing it unconditionally. The image clearly shows exposed steel members and black bands, while canvas composition and date are documented facts. A white background, black sling or steel tube alone is not a Bauhaus membership test.
