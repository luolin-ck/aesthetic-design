### Visible relationships in this exact image

- Tall repeated window bays form a largely glazed upper facade between slender brick piers.
- Glazing turns around the prominent corner instead of being enclosed by a massive solid corner block.
- A long lower wing contrasts with the taller end volume, chimney and entrance block.
- Straight roof edges, repeated bays and unadorned brick/glass surfaces organize the building's exterior.
