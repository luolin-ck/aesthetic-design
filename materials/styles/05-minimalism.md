### Documentary context and limit

A scoped example of contemporary architectural reduction: uninterrupted wall, repeated thin frames and the intervening passage govern the visible space. Not an original Minimal Art work or an exclusive distinction from Modernism.

One empty passage between the glass skin and concrete volume, not a complete building exterior, floor plan, or whole exhibition. The far-end green exit sign and small fire-safety fixture remain but are minor, normal functional elements. Natural outdoor views, surface marks and strong frame shadows remain. Do not infer all hidden structure or claim this is 1960s American Minimal Art. Architectural reduction overlaps broader Modernism; the complementary Judd image supplies a separate historical Minimal Art branch.
