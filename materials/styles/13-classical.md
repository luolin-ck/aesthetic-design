### Visible relationships in this exact image

- Fluted columns with simple rounded capitals carry a continuous horizontal entablature.
- Repeated column openings form a regular support-and-void sequence.
- The upper frieze alternates narrow grooved triglyphs and broader relief-bearing metope fields.
- The stepped base, vertical shafts and horizontal roof-edge layers form a visible base-support-crowning sequence.
