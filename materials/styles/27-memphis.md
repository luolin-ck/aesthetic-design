## Distinguishing the nearby explanations

- Shared appearances: Primary colors and geometric parts overlap with De Stijl, Pop and digital neobrutalism.
- Inspectable distinction: Carlton has an approximately bilateral overall frame with angled supports and experimental color/laminate component relations. Its documented Memphis design identity does not depend on an asymmetric total silhouette.
- Nearby explanations: De Stijl geometric furniture, Maximalism, generic colorful geometric design.
