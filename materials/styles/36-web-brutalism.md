## Distinguishing the nearby explanations

- Shared appearances: Plain documents overlap with legacy pages and reduction; bright outlined UI overlaps with Memphis-like graphics.
- Inspectable distinction: Gumroad organizes a neobrutalist interface through heavy functional boundaries and flat color fields; the Copeland page uses a direct document hierarchy, exposed links and restrained chrome. - Nearby explanations: ordinary unstyled/legacy websites, Minimalism, Memphis-like color blocking, Antidesign.
