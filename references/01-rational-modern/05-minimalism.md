# Minimalism: Design Philosophy and Style Guide

- **Name**: Minimalism
- **Concept type**: The art movement emerging in the 1960s, and broader contemporary design orientations based on reduction
- **Core definition**: Deliberately reduce competing visual and structural elements so that proportion, intervals, surfaces, light, and the core task become perceptually central.

## Attribution Criteria

### 1. Scope and Claims
- **Historical American Minimal Art of the 1960s**: Verify artist, date, exhibitions, and scholarship. Few elements and simple forms do not automatically establish movement identity.
- **Contemporary minimalist language**: This broader working category covers branding, graphics, interfaces, and spaces. Assess how reduced vocabulary organizes proportion, intervals, and materials. It neither authenticates a historical work nor reveals a creator's philosophy.

### 2. Core Evidence Dimensions

| Core evidence | How to observe or verify | Why it distinguishes | Limits and alternative explanations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Types of elements and internal variation are consistently reduced | Map principal objects, graphic types, type levels, colors, and surfaces; examine convergence toward a few basic units. | A restricted vocabulary organizes the whole, beyond a photograph of one empty wall. | Unfinished rooms, unloaded web images, and budget austerity can look sparse. There is no numerical pass mark. |
| Relationships among remaining elements carry expression | Mark intervals, sequences, ratios, junctions, reflections, and textures; ask what changing one gap or dimension would do. | Units and surrounding voids assume visual focus; empty space is active rather than waiting to be filled. | Precision and elegance are quality judgments. Rough execution can still reveal reduction and relational order. Saturated colors, including Judd's metal works, do not refute Minimalism. |
| Reduction and relationship rules extend across the assessed object | Inspect full pages, brand extensions, or room panoramas/circulation rather than an empty hero or corner. | Prevents a fragment standing for a complex whole. | An independent sculpture need not behave like a commercial brand system. Restrict conclusions to available views. |

- **Supporting appearances**: White, black, beige/gray, tiny type, expensive stone, and empty rooms are neither necessary nor sufficient. Low-contrast text and hidden actions are usability defects, not positive style criteria; poor usability also cannot cancel observable minimalist language.

### 3. Combined Judgment and Neighboring Styles
1. Identify whether the question concerns historical Minimal Art or contemporary form.
2. Reduced vocabulary plus a whole governed by intervals, proportions, or repeated sequences supports **Primary style**. An empty book cover with ornate, illustrated interiors supports **Local borrowing** or **Mixed** by area. An unfilled shell means **Insufficient evidence** of the finished state.
3. Compare [Wabi-sabi](../02-eastern-traditions/12-wabi-sabi.md): is natural weathering, unevenness, damage, and time central, or simplified units, uniform surfaces, and precise intervals? Beige and wood do not distinguish them.
4. Compare [Modernism](04-modernism.md): contemporary minimalist form need not depend on mass production or social reform.
5. Repeated layers of competing patterns, carving, and ornaments counter minimalist dominance. One partly decorated chair within a whole governed by planes and voids does not veto the entire space.

### 4. Worked Attribution Examples

| Case | Input facts → conclusion | Evidence and unresolved questions |
|---|---|---|
| Positive: Donald Judd, Untitled, 1967 | MoMA documents artist, date, and industrial production. Identical metal boxes form a vertical wall sequence; gaps equal box height; green lacquered sides contrast with bare metal → historical Minimal Art identity is established, with repeated units and equal voids as the subject. | Neither low object count nor luxury establishes attribution. Boxes and gaps jointly organize the work; vivid green is not counterevidence. Manufacture and dating depend on records, not photographs. |
| Negative, invented | A completed property-sales lobby has white walls and pale stone floors, but a full view shows chandeliers, carved sofas, layered inlaid carpets, and gilded objects governing attention → **Attribution not supported** for the whole. | Ornamental accumulation dominates; voids do not organize perception. A pre-furnishing wall crop would instead mean Insufficient evidence about the finished lobby. |
| Boundary, invented | A ceramic artist's site uses one sans-serif family, few images, and continuous voids. Object closeups and text emphasize crackle, firing distortion, and gold-repair histories → minimalist interface structure with [Wabi-sabi](../02-eastern-traditions/12-wabi-sabi.md)-oriented object narrative: **Mixed** across layers. | Layout and object narrative each have independent evidence. Beige ceramics do not make the typography Wabi-sabi. Replacing the ceramics/stories with industrial glass weakens the content claim without changing minimalist layout. |

## Position and Background

[MoMA's overview](https://www.moma.org/momaorg/shared/flattened_pages/moma_learning/themes/minimalism/) situates Minimalism in 1960s America. Artists moved away from representation and personal gestures toward geometry, prefabrication, repetition, and actual physical surroundings. Today's commercial, interior, and digital uses are broader: borrowing reduction, proportion, and active voids does not make all simple designs members of that historical movement.

## Design Philosophy

1. **Reduce competition; strengthen essential relations**: Remove distracting redundancy while ensuring each remaining element helps organize the whole.
2. **Let materials, edges, and voids express**: Removing ornament exposes texture, joints, gaps, and diffuse light—and also makes craft and alignment errors more visible.
3. **Build sequences rather than polish isolated pieces**: Simple units and precise gaps jointly create presence. Tension comes from relational control, not a contest to retain fewer things.

## Design Language

> Contemporary applications are translations for graphics, products, and spaces, not historical prescriptions for type or web components.

| Dimension | Typical language | Contemporary translation |
|---|---|---|
| Composition and layout | Few subjects, clear intervals, repeated sequences or continuous voids | Give each screen/area an unambiguous reading focus. |
| Color | Monochrome/achromatic systems and bright industrial colors/reflections | Choose by contrast and function, without black-and-white dogma. |
| Typography | Few families and type levels in contemporary graphics | Remove decorative variation while retaining scannable headings, text, and action labels. |
| Graphics and symbols | Basic geometry, linear repetition, restrained images | Give each image one clear information or emotional role; remove filler. |
| Materials and texture | Metal, acrylic, concrete, controlled stone/wood surfaces | Choose materials for touch and maintenance; refine joints and edges. |
| Space and interaction | Objects, voids, and viewer positions jointly create perception | Keep digital entrances discoverable; preserve real activity space and hidden storage. |

## Practical Design Methods

1. Define one core task, then make each element justify remaining. Remove it only if communication and structural balance survive, rather than imposing button/furniture quotas.
2. Test real text/data before deciding large voids. Gaps should group and guide; reallocate space if they cause blind scrolling or hide essentials.
3. Put saved ornament effort into tracking, crops, joints, light color rendering, stain resistance, and durability. Simpler appearance does not mean easier or cheaper manufacture.
4. Retain explicit labels, readable contrast, form errors, and keyboard focus. Plan sockets and storage for long-term living/working, beyond a temporarily tidy photograph.

## Design Example

> **Contemporary translation, invented: architectural photographer portfolio and booking site**
> - **Goal**: Let clients study composition/light without distraction and inquire about availability.
> - **Graphics and web**: A hero image, concise service line, and high-contrast booking link lead to project-grouped single-column portfolios, replacing competitive thumbnail grids. One sans-serif family has two weights. Booking still lists dates, prices, delivery times, and success states; input boundaries remain visible.
> - **Exhibition**: Arrange photographs by print size, gaps, and viewing distance without decorative backdrops. Wall voids and photographs jointly establish the exhibition rhythm.

## Representative Cases and Application Boundaries

- **Case**: Judd's 1967 [Untitled](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/81324) combines factory-made metal boxes, green automotive paint, and gaps equal to box height. Industrial color and repetition activate physical space; the voids are an inseparable half of its structure, contradicting the equation of minimalism with grayscale coldness.
- **Boundaries**: Useful for art, precision products, architectural qualities, or a single task. Public services, medical/legal explanations, and financial dashboards must retain necessary text and operations. Grids and gaps may reduce noise but cannot justify deleting business-critical information.

## Related Styles and Common Misreadings

- **With [Wabi-sabi](../02-eastern-traditions/12-wabi-sabi.md)**: Shared restraint differs in emphasis: acceptance of imperfection, hand traces, and impermanence versus Minimal Art's frequent industrial precision, uniformity, and impersonal sequences.
- **With [Modernism](04-modernism.md)**: Geometry and reduced ornament overlap, but modernism's social housing, standardization, and functional design form a broader historical program.
- **Misreading**: Hidden menus, tiny type, and pale-gray text are not required. Check reduced vocabulary and interval/proportion organization for attribution, then readability independently. Do not glorify defects or make usability a membership gate.
