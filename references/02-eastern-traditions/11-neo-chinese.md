# Neo-Chinese: Design Philosophy and Style Guide

- **Search terms**: Contemporary Chinese design / New Chinese style
- **Concept type**: A broad contemporary practice and commercial label across interiors, furniture, branding, and graphics
- **Core definition**: Reorganize living Chinese spatial, structural, craft, or graphic traditions for contemporary scales/media, beyond pasting symbols onto standard modern products.

## Attribution Criteria

### 1. Scope and Claims
Neo-Chinese is neither a dynasty nor an officially certified category. This working label means traditional Chinese visual/spatial relationships are contemporarily reorganized and become the principal organization of a work.
- **Multiple media qualify**: Courtyard sequences, furniture proportions, patterned graphic divisions, title labels, and character/image relations can all qualify. Graphics need not lose ornament and resemble timber construction. Precious wood, physical tenons, and high cost are not cross-media requirements.
- **Form versus causal process**: Compare a result with known architecture, objects, or painting/book layouts without first obtaining a designer interview. Claims of actual influence from one artifact or continuation of a heritage craft require sketches or production archives.
- **Attribution versus quality**: Neatness, comfort, and conversion concern quality/usability. Excellence does not automatically qualify, and immature translation does not automatically disqualify.

### 2. Core Evidence Dimensions

| Core evidence | How to observe or verify | Why it distinguishes | Limits and alternative explanations |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identifiable Chinese vocabulary and internal relationships | Compare framed views/courtyards/lanes, furniture crests/legs/frames, or lattice divisions/book labels/reserves/character-image connections. | Specifies what Chinese tradition means here, beyond wood, beige, and voids. | Circles, grids, wood, and symmetry cross cultures. Unknown motif dates should stay unverified rather than acquire invented stories. |
| Traditional relationships reorganized for contemporary media/use | Identify retained relationships and changed scale, rhythm, materials, or information—for example lattice divisions becoming a multi-column poster. | Distinguishes translation from unchanged replica or isolated knot icon. Reorganized ornament can itself carry structure. | Comparable form does not prove subjective inspiration. Actual weaving/lacquer production needs supply-chain/workshop records. |
| Reorganized relationships govern the main work | Examine panoramas, complete packaging, full webpages, or poster series for recurring governing relations and integration with functions. | Separates primary Neo-Chinese from one symbol in a standard modernist system. | A distinctive entrance or hero establishes only that area until the larger space/site is examined. |

- **Supporting appearances**: Wood tones, beige/ochre/vermilion, brush text, ink landscapes, moon gates, bamboo, brass, and joint closeups are search leads, not a three-or-four-item checklist. Printed graphics and digital interfaces do not need furniture materials/joints.

### 3. Combined Judgment and Neighboring Styles
1. Identify medium, then a specific Chinese relationship, then its contemporary reorganization through the main work.
2. Identifiable tradition plus governing reorganization supports **Primary style**. A logo/corner emblem alone supports **Local borrowing**. Displaying an ancient artifact photograph does not transform the surrounding layout.
3. “Eastern atmosphere” with only wood and blank space cannot distinguish Chinese, Japanese, or generic minimalism: **Insufficient evidence**, identifying needed references/full views.
4. Compare [Japandi](../04-materials-and-everyday-life/21-japandi.md): is the logic Chinese, or Japanese residential elements combined with Nordic furniture? Beige/wood cannot decide. Different functional areas may support **Mixed** by layer.
5. Compare [Ming-style](09-ming-style.md), [Literati Ink](08-literati-ink.md), and [Imperial Court Decoration](10-imperial-court-decoration.md): specific traditions differ from this umbrella. Distinguish intact furniture types from lines extracted into new products/layouts.

Graphic ornament can accomplish translation independently of expensive craft. Without sketches, establish formal correspondence rather than subjective inspiration. |
| Negative, invented | A medical booking page uses generic three-column rounded cards and sans-serif type, with one red seal-shaped profile icon → **Attribution not supported** for the whole; Local borrowing only. | Replacing the icon with a person leaves the governing grid intact. ## Position and Background

There is no universally accepted founding manifesto; architecture, furniture, fashion, and branding use the term differently. This guide adopts “contemporary translation of Chinese traditional vocabulary,” not a universal template.

Jiang Qiong Er's [SHANG XIA account](https://www.jiangqionger.com/project/project-shang-xia) joins bamboo weaving, thin porcelain, and felt to daily objects. Neri&Hu's [Waterhouse at South Bund](https://www.neriandhu.com/zn/works/the-vertical-lane-house-the-waterhouse-at-south-bund) juxtaposes old/new materials and translates Shanghai lanes' public/private intertwining into vertical hotel circulation. These are method examples for craft and local memory, not claims that the makers endorse the commercial Neo-Chinese label.

## Design Philosophy

1. **Inherit relations of structure, space, and behavior**: Construction, hospitality sequences, framed views, touch, and daily actions can carry tradition without filling surfaces with auspicious motifs.
2. **Serve present conditions**: Ceiling heights, cleaning, mass-production quality, and touchscreens require alteration. Painful seating, difficult cleaning, and unreadable text are not cultural atmosphere.
3. **Explain source and change**: Name the craft or corridor sequence and the adjustments made for current functions, beyond empty Eastern slogans.

## Design Language

> These are selectable contemporary paths; no single dimension is universally mandatory.

| Dimension | Typical language | Contemporary translation |
|---|---|---|
| Composition and layout | Framed/opposed views, courtyard depth, painting voids, book-label columns | Evaluate actual reading efficiency or circulation after translation. |
| Color | Kiln glazes, local minerals/plant dyes, timber/stone | Ground colors in named objects/places while fitting identity needs. |
| Typography | Square character structure, vertical/horizontal rhythm, modern printing | Keep body text readable; expressive lettering must connect structurally and semantically. |
| Graphics and symbols | Abstract landscapes, lattices, object motifs, woodworking joints | Retain meaningful connections after flattening/geometric simplification. |
| Materials and texture | Bamboo, lacquer, ceramic, silk with metal, glass, composites, industry | Test durability, maintenance, and craft honesty; fake woodgrain plastic is not a substitute for a grounded proposition. |
| Space and interaction | Contemporary courtyards, changing corridor views, neighborly lanes | Meet privacy, daylight, ventilation, accessibility, and intuitive digital operation. |

## Practical Design Methods

1. Focus on one concrete lineage, such as Jiangnan bamboo intersections or Shanghai lane light/dark transitions; do not pile Ming chairs, Qing dragons, Ru ware, and dry-brush effects together.
2. Tabulate retained relationships, changed sizes/materials, and user problems. Verify with prototypes and real text rather than atmospheric renders alone.
3. Make reusable medium-specific rules: grids/type/graphics for branding; living/social/storage behavior for interiors; ergonomics/climate stability for furniture.
4. Separate visual expression from modern interaction. Heroes, rhythm, and material photography may carry style, while navigation, search, purchasing, errors, and hit targets follow current HCI requirements.

## Design Example

> **Contemporary translation, invented: local heritage bamboo-weaving cooperative**
> - **Goal**: Bring a century-old rural cooperative into urban homeware markets with honest craft and clear purchasing.
> - **Packaging/graphics**: Hexagonal holes and herringbone intersections become information grids and sealing bands, without generic ink/seals. Labels list artisan, bamboo harvest year, village, and moisture-care guidance in readable sans-serif; natural-light macros show fibers.
> - **Space/web**: Reusable modular woven screens divide a pop-up, echoing traditional reveal/conceal while assembling quickly. The website diagrams splitting bamboo, forming the base, weaving, and finishing; cart/checkout remain efficient.

## Representative Cases and Application Boundaries

- **Cases**: [SHANG XIA](https://www.jiangqionger.com/project/project-shang-xia) translates bamboo-wrapped porcelain and hand-felted cashmere into contemporary furniture/objects. [Waterhouse](https://www.neriandhu.com/zn/works/the-vertical-lane-house-the-waterhouse-at-south-bund) retains weathered concrete and uses weathering steel/glass for vertical lane experiences. Craft in daily life and local spatial memory are different paths, neither a universal template.
- **Boundaries**: Suitable for cultural brands, homes, hospitality, craft revitalization, and locally identifiable exhibitions. Heritage repair, archaeological reconstruction, and religious ritual sites require conservation/authenticity standards; creative adaptation cannot justify damaging remains.

## Related Styles and Common Misreadings

- **With [Ming-style](09-ming-style.md), [Literati Ink](08-literati-ink.md), and [Imperial Court Decoration](10-imperial-court-decoration.md)**: Specific historical types/contexts supply sources; Neo-Chinese is a present-facing translation framework.
- **With [Japandi](../04-materials-and-everyday-life/21-japandi.md)**: Shared linen, wood, and beige do not equate Chinese reorganization with Japanese/Nordic functional fusion.
- **Misreading**: Standard layout plus brush title, red accent, or false moon gate is insufficient. Traditional relationships must be reorganized and govern the main work; graphics can do so through patterns/labels without physical joints. Practical quality remains independent.
